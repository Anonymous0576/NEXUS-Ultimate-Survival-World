One world. Countless ways to play.

NEXUS brings survival, action, exploration, racing, sports, and creativity together in one massive multiplayer experience.

Travel from bustling cities to quiet villages, dense forests, sun-scorched deserts, remote islands, and hidden locations. Venture out alone or join friends to explore a world that keeps changing around you.

Take on a mission, gather resources, compete for victory, or simply make your own fun. In NEXUS, the next adventure is yours to choose.
